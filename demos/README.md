# Demonstration editing notes

`bottle_to_mousepad/demo_4x.mp4`: 236.00s, 720×1280, 30fps, silent. Selected from a 4704.37s recording. Chronological selection of 117 intervals, approximately 944.36s of source footage; every retained interval is 4×, including the opening and ending. No artificial stills. Observation/waiting gaps and idle tail removed. Stage captions and original-video time intervals are visible. `timeline.csv` records the source-to-edit mapping. The video was fully decoded and selected output frames reviewed before packaging.

`paper_partial_placement/demo_4x.mp4`: 83.03s, 1280×720, 30fps, silent. Uniform 4× across 17 retained intervals; observation/waiting removed. Paper only partly reaches the mat; the caption preserves that limitation. No artificial stills.

Demonstration duration is neither original recording duration nor measured physical-motion time. Robot-event logs and camera footage were not established as hardware synchronized. For the bottle edit, an approximate time offset was used for localization and checked visually.

No original audio is included in these two selected clips. Videos show the laboratory environment; the original long recordings and continuous multi-camera streams are excluded. Original clips and accompanying posters are project documentation under the root MIT license.
